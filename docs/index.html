From our field desk

Every case starts in the paper. Open the game and it reads the paper for the state you're standing in, with real local legends behind each headline: the Jersey Devil in the Pine Barrens, Mothman at Point Pleasant, the lake serpent of Flathead.

Pick one, put the phone in your pocket, and start walking. Any direction will do. The game doesn't send you to a map pin. The trail finds you.

How a case goes

Walk. Listen. Name it. Get the shot.

A case takes a short walk. Around the block works, and so does a trail. On screen you see a hand-drawn first-person scene of the place where the sighting happened.

  1. Take a case

    Choose a sighting from your state's paper. Each one names a place and a story, never the creature.

  2. Walk for clues

    Head out in any direction. Every few hundred steps something turns up, and you'll hear a few rising bells when it does.

  3. Narrow it down

    Check each clue against the field guide. You get one guess per clue, and the case runs hotter or colder after each, without saying which guess was right.

  4. Bait the trail

    Pick up lures as you search and set one out. The right one draws it closer. The wrong one sets you back.

  5. Get the photo

    It breaks cover. Turn to keep it in the frame, and shoot. Every shot is scored and filed in your journal.

In the field

Built to be played outside

Your sky, your weather
The scene matches the real time of day and the weather where you are, so a rainy dusk walk looks like one.
Music made as you walk
The soundtrack isn't a recording. A low drone, slow minor chords and stray music-box bells are made up as it plays, and a heartbeat comes in when something's close.
Know the call
Creatures make the same sound their recordings do. If you've studied the tape, you might know what's moving in the brush before you see it.
A journal of every case
Closed cases go in your field journal, with your best photo of each creature and every shot from the encounter.
Traveler mode
Read another state's paper and work its cases from home. You still have to walk for the clues.
No account, no sign-up
Install and walk. Your journal stays on your phone.
